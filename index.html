What the numbers mean

Reading grade
Flesch-Kincaid: the US school grade a reader needs, from words per sentence and syllables per word.
Reading ease
Flesch reading ease, 0 to 100; higher is easier.
Richness (TTR)
Unique words divided by total words. Lower means more repetition, and it falls as a text gets longer.
Coverage curve
How few distinct words you need to follow most of the book. I used the same measure on reality TV in the Love Island lexical analysis.
